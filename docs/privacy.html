Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Fiscus Board is a budgeting app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your budget stays on your device, and we don't collect it.

Information we collect

None. Fiscus Board doesn't have accounts, and it doesn't collect, store or transmit your personal information or your budget to us or anyone else.

Your budget data

Everything you enter, including income, expenses, sections, settings and review progress, is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. It is not uploaded to any server. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

If you use Restore from backup, you paste backup text into the app yourself. That text is read on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Fiscus Board contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Third parties

We don't sell, share or give your information to third parties, because we don't receive it. Apple may collect information when you download apps or use TestFlight, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Fiscus Board isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds optional cloud backup, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@fiscusboard.app
