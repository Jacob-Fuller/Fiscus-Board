Fiscus Board is a simple, private budget laid out like a spreadsheet and built around your pay cycle.

Add your income and bills once, at whatever frequency they really happen – weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly or yearly – and Fiscus Board converts everything to what it costs each pay. The total card shows what's left each fortnight, and what that adds up to over a year.

LAID OUT CLEARLY
• Sections and subheadings, with totals for each
• Item, amount and how often, with the per-pay figure beside it
• View everything per week, fortnight or month

SHARED COSTS
• Split a section between 2 to 6 people
• See the full total and your share; only your share counts toward your budget

INSIGHTS
• Where your pay goes, section by section
• Your biggest expenses
• In, out and left over per week, fortnight, month and year

QUARTERLY REVIEW
• An optional check-in every 3 months
• Tick off each amount as you confirm it, and the next review is set automatically

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your budget is stored only on your iPhone
• Hide amounts with one tap when others are around

MAKE IT YOURS
• Light or dark appearance
• Seven accent colours
• Name your budget
